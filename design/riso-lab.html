Riso, four ways

Same Riso idea (two inks out of register, halftone, hard shadows), four different takes. Every phone is live, and the picker switches all four at once. Turn the sound on, burn the fuse, hold the impostor card.

A: the one you picked

Classic

Pink and blue on white, fat wide caps. The baseline.

Palette

Type
Unbounded caps, Figtree body.
Good
Loud and instantly recognisable.
Weak spot
All caps in a wide face makes long words ("Pohádkové postavy") wrap early, and it shouts all evening.
B: for dim rooms

Night

The same print on black stock. Inks glow instead of overprinting.

Palette
black stockfluoro pinkmintyellowwhite ink
Type
Same as Classic.
Good
No glare across a dark table, and it looks like a gig poster. The overlaps brighten instead of darkening.
Weak spot
Weaker in daylight. Best used as the dark mode of A or D, switched by the phone's own setting.
C: Czech film poster

Plakát

Orange and teal on newsprint, tall condensed letters like a 60s cinema poster.

Palette
newsprintfluoro orangetealyellowink
Type
BIG SHOULDERS DISPLAY 900, Archivo body.
Good
Condensed type means the word on the card can be 50% bigger, which is the best for reading across a table. It also brings back the Czech flavour.
Weak spot
Feels more retro and less "party". The orange and teal pair is calmer than pink and blue.
D: a colour per game

Barevné hry

Each game prints in its own ink pair. Sentence case, rounder corners, friendlier.

Ink pairs
KloboukImpostorBombaNa čele
Type
Bricolage Grotesque in sentence case, Figtree body.
Good
You know which game you're in from across the room, and it scales: a new game just gets a new ink pair. Sentence case reads faster than caps.
Weak spot
Four palettes to keep balanced. The home screen is busier.

My pick

D for the light theme, B as its dark mode.

Colour per game is the one idea here that makes the app easier to use, not just nicer to look at, and sentence case calms down the shouting that will wear on you after an hour. Night is the same system on black, so you'd get a proper dark mode for late games for almost free. If you love the condensed type in C, I can use it for the big word on the cards only. That's the one place where size matters most.
